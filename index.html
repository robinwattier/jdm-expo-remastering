From Japanese auction bidding and mechanical inspection in Nagoya to international customs clearance and front-door delivery.

1. Japanese Auction Access

Direct access to 100+ Japanese auto auction halls (USS Tokyo, TAA, CAA, JU). We translate auction inspection sheets, verify condition grades, and bid live on your behalf.

2. In-House Reconditioning

Our Yatomi-shi workshop performs comprehensive fluid flushes, rust-prevention undercoating, compression checks, and NISMO/aftermarket tuning according to your specs.

We handle original Japanese Export Certificates, official English translations, de-registration titles, EPA Form 3520-1, DOT Form HS-7, and verified odometer certificates.

RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off) and dedicated container shipping from Nagoya, Yokohama, and Kobe ports directly to major ports in the USA, Canada, UK, Europe, Australia, and New Zealand.

KNOWLEDGE BASE

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential information regarding Japanese car export, legal rules, payments, and international logistics.

What is the 25-Year Rule for importing JDM cars to the USA?

Under US Federal Law (NHTSA & EPA regulations), vehicles that are 25 calendar years old or older calculated from the month and year of original manufacture are fully exempt from Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards (FMVSS) and EPA emissions requirements. This allows 100% legal road registration across all 50 states.

Can I visit the Yatomi-shi facility in Nagoya before purchasing?

Yes! We actively welcome clients from all over the world to visit our Nagoya / Yatomi-shi headquarters during business hours (Mon-Sat 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM JST). You can inspect your target vehicle, test equipment, review documentation, and meet our engineering team in person.

How does the Japanese Car Auction bidding service work?

If the vehicle you seek is not in our direct showroom inventory, we grant you access to our live portal connected to 100+ Japanese auctions (USS, CAA, TAA, etc.). We translate official auction inspection sheets, provide professional price guidance, inspect vehicles on-site when possible, and bid live on your authorization.

What payment methods and export currencies are accepted?

We accept international bank wire transfers (Telegraphic Transfer / T.T.) in USD, JPY, and EUR to our corporate banking accounts in Japan. We also offer secure Escrow solutions for high-value collector vehicles.
